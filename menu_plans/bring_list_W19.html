Instructions

  1. Monday lunch: Spinach and Mushroom Quiche with Side of Mixed Greens (Balsamic Dressing) + Whole Grain Baguette
  2. Monday dinner: Grilled Halloumi and Watermelon Salad with Mint, Cucumber, and Pomegranate Seeds + Side of Crusty Bread
  3. Tuesday lunch: Picard Hachis Parmentier (Frozen Meal) with Side of Sautéed Green Beans and Cherry Tomatoes
  4. Tuesday dinner: Grilled Chicken Paillard with Lemon-Caper Sauce, Roasted New Potatoes, and Steamed Asparagus
  5. Wednesday lunch: Crispy Tofu Banh Mi with Pickled Carrots, Daikon, Cucumber, and Sriracha Mayo + Side of Sweet Potato Fries
  6. Wednesday dinner: Seared Scallops with Garlic Butter Sauce, Risotto alla Milanese, and Sautéed Spinach
  7. Thursday lunch: Mediterranean Chickpea-Free Panisse Burger with Roasted Red Pepper Tapenade, Arugula, and Mozzarella + Side of Mixed Greens
  8. Thursday dinner: Picard Boeuf Bourguignon (Frozen Meal) with Side of Buttered Egg Noodles and Steamed Broccoli
  9. Friday lunch: Niçoise-Style Salad with Seared Tuna, Green Beans, Cherry Tomatoes, Olives, and Hard-Boiled Eggs + Whole Grain Baguette
  10. Friday dinner: Pork and Apple Stuffed Crepes with Side of Mixed Greens (Dijon Vinaigrette)
