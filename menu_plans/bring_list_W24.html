Instructions

  1. Monday lunch: Caprese Salad with Heirloom Tomatoes, Fresh Basil, and Balsamic Glaze + Side of Grilled Ciabatta
  2. Monday dinner: Spinach and Ricotta Stuffed Crepes with Mushroom Sauce + Side of Mixed Greens (Lemon-Olive Oil Dressing)
  3. Tuesday lunch: Picard Poulet Basquaise (Frozen Meal) with Side of Steamed Green Beans and Basmati Rice
  4. Tuesday dinner: Grilled Duck Breast with Cherry-Port Sauce, Roasted Potatoes, and Sautéed Haricots Verts
  5. Wednesday lunch: Mushroom and Swiss Cheese Burger with Caramelized Onions and Garlic Aioli + Side of Sweet Potato Fries
  6. Wednesday dinner: Vietnamese Summer Rolls with Shrimp, Rice Vermicelli, Lettuce, Mint, and Peanut Dipping Sauce
  7. Thursday lunch: Grilled Polenta with Roasted Cherry Tomatoes, Arugula, and Shaved Parmesan + Side of Grilled Zucchini
  8. Thursday dinner: Picard Lasagnes à la Bolognaise (Frozen Meal) with Side of Garlic Bread and Mixed Greens (Balsamic Dressing)
  9. Friday lunch: Niçoise Salad with Seared Tuna, Green Beans, Cherry Tomatoes, Olives, Hard-Boiled Eggs, and Anchovies + Side of Whole Grain Baguette
  10. Friday dinner: Grilled Swordfish with Lemon-Caper Butter, Couscous, and Ratatouille
