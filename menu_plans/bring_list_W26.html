Weekly Recipe (schema.org)

Ingredients

  • zucchini (2 units)
  • cherry tomatoes (500g)
  • mixed greens (200g)
  • green beans (300g)
  • haricots verts (300g)
  • sweet potato (2 units)
  • asparagus (300g)
  • cucumber (2 units)
  • arugula (100g)
  • carrots (4 units)
  • spinach (200g)
  • eggplant (1 unit)
  • bell pepper (2 units)
  • onion (2 units)
  • garlic (1 head)
  • pork tenderloin (300g)
  • duck confit (2 legs)
  • salmon fillets (2 units)
  • gruyère (100g)
  • fontina cheese (100g)
  • feta (100g)
  • mozzarella (200g)
  • butter (150g)
  • eggs (6 units)
  • whole grain baguette (1 unit)
  • ciabatta (1 unit)
  • quinoa (200g)
  • orzo (200g)
  • egg noodles (200g)
  • pita bread (2 units)
  • kalamata olives (100g)
  • truffle aioli (1 jar)
  • lemon (4 units)
  • olive oil (1 bottle)
  • balsamic dressing (1 bottle)
  • honey (1 jar)
  • mustard (1 jar)
  • pesto (1 jar)
  • dill (1 bunch)
  • Picard Saucisses de Toulouse aux Lentilles (1 meal)
  • Picard Boeuf à la Mode (1 meal)

Instructions

  1. Monday lunch: Provençal Omelette with Cherry Tomatoes, Zucchini, and Gruyère + Side of Mixed Greens (Balsamic Dressing) + Whole Grain Baguette
  2. Monday dinner: Ratatouille Niçoise with Poached Eggs and Grilled Ciabatta
  3. Tuesday lunch: Picard Saucisses de Toulouse aux Lentilles (Frozen Meal) with Side of Buttered Mashed Potatoes and Sautéed Green Beans
  4. Tuesday dinner: Grilled Pork Tenderloin with Mustard-Honey Glaze, Roasted Sweet Potatoes, and Sautéed Haricots Verts
  5. Wednesday lunch: Mushroom and Fontina Cheese Burger with Caramelized Onions and Truffle Aioli + Side of Arugula Salad (Lemon-Olive Oil Dressing)
  6. Wednesday dinner: Pan-Seared Salmon with Lemon-Dill Sauce, Quinoa, and Steamed Asparagus
  7. Thursday lunch: Greek-Style Orzo Salad with Cucumber, Cherry Tomatoes, Kalamata Olives, Red Onion, and Feta + Side of Grilled Pita Bread
  8. Thursday dinner: Picard Boeuf à la Mode (Frozen Meal) with Side of Buttered Egg Noodles and Roasted Carrots
  9. Friday lunch: Grilled Vegetable and Mozzarella Panini with Pesto Drizzle + Side of Roasted Cherry Tomatoes and Mixed Greens
  10. Friday dinner: Duck Confit with Pommes Sarladaises (Potatoes with Garlic and Duck Fat) and Sautéed Spinach
