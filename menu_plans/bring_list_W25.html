Instructions

  8. Thursday dinner: Picard Poulet Rôti aux Herbes (Frozen Meal) with Side of Roasted Potatoes and Sautéed Green Beans
  9. Friday lunch: Italian Tuna and White Bean-Free Salad with Red Onion, Capers, and Parsley + Side of Grilled Ciabatta
  10. Friday dinner: Grilled Octopus with Lemon, Garlic, and Olive Oil, Served with Orzo and Sautéed Spinach
