Weekly Recipe (schema.org)

Ingredients

  • broccoli (300g)
  • carrots (400g)

  • lettuce (1 head)
  • tomato (3 units)
  • cherry tomatoes (300g)
  • arugula (100g)
  • cucumber (2 units)
  • eggplant (1 unit)
  • red peppers (2 units)
  • frisée (1 head)
  • green beans (300g)
  • pork tenderloin (300g)
  • shrimp (200g)
  • duck confit (2 legs)
  • eggs (6 units)

  • ricotta (250g)

  • basil pesto (1 jar)

  • balsamic dressing (1 bottle)

  • Picard Boeuf Stroganoff (1 meal)

Instructions

  1. Monday lunch: Asparagus and Pea Shoot Salad with Poached Eggs, Parmesan Shavings, and Toasted Baguette Slices
  2. Monday dinner: Courgette and Ricotta Galette with Side of Mixed Greens (Lemon-Olive Oil Dressing)
  3. Tuesday lunch: Picard Saumon en Papillote (Frozen Meal) with Side of Steamed Broccoli and Quinoa
  4. Tuesday dinner: Grilled Pork Tenderloin with Mustard Cream Sauce, Roasted Carrots, and Mashed Potatoes
  5. Wednesday lunch: Falafel and Hummus-Free Chickpea Patty Burger with Tahini Sauce, Lettuce, Tomato, and Whole Wheat Bun + Side of Sweet Potato Fries
  6. Wednesday dinner: Vietnamese Summer Rolls with Shrimp, Rice Vermicelli, Mint, and Peanut Dipping Sauce + Side of Cucumber Salad
  7. Thursday lunch: Italian Farro Salad with Roasted Cherry Tomatoes, Arugula, Mozzarella Pearls, and Basil Pesto + Grilled Halloumi
  8. Thursday dinner: Picard Boeuf Stroganoff (Frozen Meal) with Side of Steamed Green Beans and Egg Noodles
  9. Friday lunch: Grilled Vegetable Panini with Zucchini, Eggplant, Roasted Red Peppers, and Provolone Cheese + Side of Mixed Greens (Balsamic Dressing)
  10. Friday dinner: Duck Confit Salad with Frisée, Walnuts, and Cherry Tomatoes (Sherry Vinaigrette)
