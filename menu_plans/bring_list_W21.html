Instructions

  1. Monday lunch: Spinach and Ricotta Stuffed Shells with Side of Arugula Salad (Lemon-Olive Oil Dressing)
  2. Monday dinner: Grilled Zucchini and Eggplant Stacks with Burrata, Cherry Tomatoes, and Basil Pesto + Side of Toasted Baguette
  3. Tuesday lunch: Picard Cannelloni aux Trois Fromages (Frozen Meal) with Side of Sautéed Spinach and Cherry Tomatoes
  4. Tuesday dinner: Grilled Lamb Chops with Mint Yogurt Sauce, Roasted Potatoes, and Steamed Green Beans
  5. Wednesday lunch: Crispy Tempeh Burger with Avocado, Lettuce, Tomato, and Garlic Aioli + Side of Sweet Potato Fries
  6. Wednesday dinner: Thai-Inspired Coconut Curry with Shrimp, Bell Peppers, and Jasmine Rice
  7. Thursday lunch: Mediterranean Tuna and White Bean-Free Salad with Olives, Cherry Tomatoes, Red Onion, and Parsley + Side of Crusty Bread
  8. Thursday dinner: Picard Boeuf à la Bordelaise (Frozen Meal) with Side of Buttered Egg Noodles and Steamed Asparagus
  9. Friday lunch: Grilled Halloumi and Watermelon Salad with Mint, Cucumber, and Pomegranate Seeds + Side of Whole Grain Baguette
  10. Friday dinner: Seared Salmon with Lemon-Dill Sauce, Quinoa, and Sautéed Swiss Chard
