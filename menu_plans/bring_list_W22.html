Instructions

  1. Monday lunch: Mozzarella and Tomato Panini with Pesto Drizzle + Side of Roasted Zucchini and Cherry Tomatoes
  2. Monday dinner: Provençal Vegetable Tian with Poached Eggs and Side of Mixed Greens (Lemon-Olive Oil Dressing)
  3. Tuesday lunch: Picard Saumon en Papillote (Frozen Meal) with Side of Steamed Broccoli and Basmati Rice
  4. Tuesday dinner: Grilled Pork Tenderloin with Mustard-Honey Glaze, Roasted Sweet Potatoes, and Sautéed Green Beans
  5. Wednesday lunch: Falafel Burger with Tahini Sauce, Lettuce, Tomato, and Pickled Red Onions + Side of Carrot and Cucumber Salad
  6. Wednesday dinner: Thai Green Curry with Chicken, Bell Peppers, and Jasmine Rice
  7. Thursday lunch: Italian Farro Salad with Roasted Cherry Tomatoes, Arugula, Parmesan, and Balsamic Glaze + Grilled Halloumi
  8. Thursday dinner: Picard Boeuf aux Carottes (Frozen Meal) with Side of Buttered Egg Noodles and Steamed Asparagus
  9. Friday lunch: Grilled Vegetable and Ricotta Tartine with Side of Mixed Greens (Balsamic Dressing)
  10. Friday dinner: Seared Cod with Lemon-Caper Butter, Quinoa, and Sautéed Spinach
