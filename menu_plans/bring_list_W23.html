Instructions

  1. Monday lunch: Grilled Halloumi and Peach Salad with Rocket, Walnuts, and Honey-Mustard Dressing + Side of Whole Grain Baguette

  8. Thursday dinner: Picard Boeuf Bourguignon (Frozen Meal) with Side of Buttered Egg Noodles and Steamed Broccoli
  9. Friday lunch: Grilled Aubergine and Mozzarella Panini with Pesto Drizzle + Side of Roasted Cherry Tomatoes
