Instructions

  1. Monday lunch: Caprese Stuffed Avocado with Cherry Tomatoes, Basil, and Balsamic Glaze + Side of Whole Grain Baguette
  2. Monday dinner: Ratatouille with Poached Eggs and Side of Mixed Greens (Lemon-Olive Oil Dressing)
  3. Tuesday lunch: Picard Poulet Basquaise (Frozen Meal) with Side of Steamed Green Beans and Basmati Rice
  4. Tuesday dinner: Grilled Mackerel with Lemon-Herb Butter, Roasted New Potatoes, and Sautéed Zucchini
  5. Wednesday lunch: Portobello Mushroom Burger with Caramelized Onions, Provolone Cheese, and Garlic Aioli + Side of Sweet Potato Fries
  6. Wednesday dinner: Vietnamese Pho-Inspired Noodle Salad with Rice Noodles, Shrimp, Mint, and Peanuts
  7. Thursday lunch: Italian Orzo Salad with Roasted Cherry Tomatoes, Arugula, Mozzarella Pearls, and Basil Pesto + Grilled Halloumi
  8. Thursday dinner: Picard Lasagnes à la Bolognaise (Frozen Meal) with Side of Mixed Greens (Balsamic Dressing)
  9. Friday lunch: Grilled Vegetable and Hummus Wrap with Roasted Red Peppers, Spinach, and Feta-Free Tzatziki + Side of Cucumber Salad
  10. Friday dinner: Seared Duck Breast with Cherry Port Sauce, Polenta, and Sautéed Swiss Chard
